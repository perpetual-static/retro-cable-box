# Buy a box

A finished Retro Cable Box: assembled, tested, and ready to plug into your
TV.

<!-- generated:shop -->

## In the box

- The Retro Cable Box (Raspberry Pi 5 in the printed case, with the software
  installed and the starter lineup loaded)
- The power supply
- A micro-HDMI to HDMI cable

## You need

- A TV with an HDMI input
- Wi-Fi
- An AI agent that can use MCP servers, to program your channels. See
  [Connect your agent](connect.html).

Your TV remote works through HDMI-CEC on most TVs. For a TV without CEC, add
a USB IR receiver (see [Build the box](build.html)).

Want to build it yourself? Everything is here: the [parts and
case files](build.html) and the [software](setup.html).
